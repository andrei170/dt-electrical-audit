The Row That Decides Whether You Get Asked To Quote
On this work, accreditation is procurement, not marketing

On domestic jobs a homeowner may never ask. On commercial and industrial work, main contractors, councils, housing associations and facilities managers routinely require scheme membership and insurance levels before a firm can be added to a tender list at all.

Requirements vary by buyer and by contract, so this is not a rule we can state for your market. But every competitor we could find has decided it is worth putting first — and you are the one telling us you want more of exactly that work.

If you hold them, this is twenty minutes: logos and registration numbers on every page, plus your public liability and employers' liability cover. If you do not, that is the single most valuable conversation we can have, and it matters more than everything else in both documents combined.

Where You Are Genuinely Ahead
This field is thinner than it looks, and you hold the best card in it
  • You are the only one of the four with case studies. Not one competitor publishes any. On commercial work, where a buyer wants evidence you have done this before at this scale, that is the most persuasive asset available — and you already have it.
  • Your homepage is longer than two of the three. 755 words against 622 and 606.
  • Two of the three have almost nothing published — Binary North declares one page, SRW four. Only Livewire, at 19, has built any depth.
  • Nobody is retargeting except Livewire. One Meta pixel across the whole field.
  • You publish a real trading address. So do they — but on tender work it is table stakes and you have it.
And where they are ahead

Two of the three can see what works and you cannot. Livewire and SRW both run Google Analytics. On a trade with a long research cycle, they can see which pages a buyer reads in the weeks before they call. You have no visibility of that at all.

Livewire is the one to watch. 19 pages, analytics and a Meta pixel — the only firm here behaving like it intends to grow, and they are on your doorstep.

What We Could Not Check
Marked honestly rather than guessed
  • Which accreditations any firm here actually holds, including you. We read websites, not registers. A firm may hold more than it publishes, or publish on a page we did not read.
  • Inner pages on all four sites. We read homepages and sitemaps; accreditations are often on an “about” or “accreditations” page.
  • The Meta Ad Library refused every request we made — 403 direct, CAPTCHA through a proxy. So nothing here describes or assesses any advertisement by any firm, including yours. Livewire having a pixel installed is not evidence they advertise or of any result.
  • Everyone's Google Business Profile, reviews and map position, including yours.
  • Anyone's revenue, staffing, pricing, tender list membership or win rate, including yours.
  • Every commercial electrical contractor in Merseyside. This is the set a search returned and we could verify — not a census, and it excludes the national M&E contractors bidding the largest work.

You are the only firm here with case studies, and the only one without a badge on the page.

One of those is hard to build. The other is an afternoon.
